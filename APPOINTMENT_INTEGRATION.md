## 🎯 Features Available

When patients click "Book Appointment", they get:

- ✅ **Doctor Selection** - Browse all approved doctors with ratings and fees
- ✅ **Interactive Calendar** - Visual calendar to pick appointment date  
- ✅ **Time Slot Picker** - Choose from available 30-minute slots (9 AM - 5 PM)
- ✅ **Appointment Notes** - Add notes about the appointment
- ✅ **Real-time Validation** - Checks doctor availability before booking
- ✅ **Automated Notifications** - Doctor gets notified instantly

## 🚀 How to Use

### As a Patient:

1. **Login** to your patient account
2. **Navigate** to your dashboard (you'll see it automatically)

4. **Select** your preferred doctor
5. **Pick** a date from the calendar
6. **Choose** an available time slot
7. **Add** any notes (optional)
8. **Click** "Book Appointment"
9. **Done!** Doctor receives notification
